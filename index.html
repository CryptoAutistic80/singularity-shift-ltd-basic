Working together

A clear next step, at every stage.

You do not need a finished brief or a list of technologies.

Tell me what you want to achieve and where things stand. I’ll help turn that into a piece of work you can understand, with the important decisions made together.

  1. 01Talk through the need

    Your business, the people using it, what is getting in the way and what success would look like.

  2. 02Agree the scope and cost

    A proposal that sets out what is included, the assumptions and the proposed next step before work begins.

  3. 03See the work take shape

    Review the design and working progress, ask questions and make decisions with the person building it.

  4. 04Launch with a handover

    Check the result, understand how it works and agree any follow-on work or support you need.

Scope & pricing

The right amount of help.

Every project is priced individually. Small improvements are welcome, and larger builds can be broken into clear, useful stages.

A focused improvement

Start with one useful change.

Improve a website, fix an awkward workflow, connect two tools or test a product idea. We agree a scope that makes sense for the need.

A complete build

Bring a website or product to life.

Work together from planning and design through development and launch, with the scope, delivery stages and costs set out in the proposal.

Know what you are agreeing to.

After an initial conversation, I’ll recommend a next step and explain what is included, what is excluded and what it costs. If an existing system needs investigation, we can scope that first instead of guessing at the whole build.
