How the work moves

Fast works best when the target is clear.

Design and frontend implementation happen in the same working loop.

That removes a long hand-off, makes visual decisions concrete early and keeps a short job focused on what needs to be visible and usable.

  1. 01Show me the target

    Bring the brief, screen, current site or reference that defines the direction.

  2. 02Fix the scope

    Agree the pages, states, responsive behaviour and finish line before the build.

  3. 03Build in the browser

    Move quickly through real responsive screens instead of a long chain of hand-offs.

  4. 04Hand over cleanly

    Finish with tested code, clear ownership and the route to publish or extend it.
