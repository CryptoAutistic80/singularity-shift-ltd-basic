Using the app

PDF: Easy sign pro is provided by Singularity Shift. You may use it for personal or business documents you are authorised to access and sign. Your documents and signing marks remain yours. Contact: singularityshiftai@gmail.com.

Signing and review

The app places visible electronic signing marks and fills supported PDF fields. It does not verify identity, apply a certificate-backed digital signature, provide legal advice or guarantee recipient acceptance. Check any required signing method with the recipient. Do not use another person’s signature or stamp without authority. Review your details, placement and saved output. The review workflow does not determine that every required field is complete.

Compatibility and keeping copies

Some PDF types and features are unsupported. Keep copies of important originals and finished documents. If a file fails, retain the original and contact support with a description of the issue before sharing confidential content.

Free and Pro

Basic signing, filling, stamps, navigation, pen styles, saving and sharing are free. Pro offers the same extra repeat-work features without ads through either a one-time lifetime purchase or an optional monthly subscription. Google Play shows the local price before payment. Monthly Pro renews automatically until cancelled in Google Play. Cancellation stops future renewal and access continues through the paid entitlement. A lifetime purchase does not automatically cancel a separate monthly subscription; manage that subscription in Google Play to stop further charges. Saved data and edits already applied to documents are retained when Pro access ends. Restore purchases using the purchasing Google Play account. Google Play’s purchasing rules and applicable consumer rights govern payments and refunds. Contact us if paid features are missing or do not work as described.

Optional ad rewards

You may choose to watch an ad to earn bulk-initials access for the current source document on this device. The app grants access when the ad service confirms the reward, and retains it for reopening, drafts, recovery and save retries. This is not paid Pro access. Availability depends on the ad service and connection; manual signing and clean export remain available if you decline or an ad fails. Earned records can be deleted in Privacy or by removing app data. They are not transferred to another device. Exported PDFs and already placed edits remain available.
