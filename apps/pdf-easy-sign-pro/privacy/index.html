Who we are

PDF: Easy sign pro is a Singularity Shift app for signing and filling PDFs on Android. For support or privacy enquiries, email singularityshiftai@gmail.com. This policy concerns the app; services you choose alongside it have their own policies.

Your documents

PDFs, signatures, initials, stamp images, form values and profiles are processed on your device. The app does not upload them to a Singularity Shift document service. There is no app account or app-operated document analytics service. Optional advertising and Google Play use network connections and process their own service data as described below. The app does not use your documents to train AI models.

Active work and saved copies

You select files through Android or open a PDF from another app. The app creates a temporary working copy and page previews in private storage. Active files are not all encrypted by the app. Cleanup removes them when the session is completed or discarded, or at the next app startup after an interruption. Cleanup failures are reported for retry. Deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure. Original files stay unchanged; exported PDFs are separate copies in the location you choose.

Optional saved data

You can save reusable signing marks, a company stamp, drafts, recent-file history, and Pro profiles or layouts. These saved records are encrypted using Android Keystore-managed keys. They remain until deleted, replaced, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Recent-file history keeps up to eight names and file links. It retains Android read access for remembered files; removing entries relinquishes that retained access without deleting originals.

Automatic recovery

Recovery is off until enabled. It can keep five encrypted sessions containing the source PDF, additions, form values and unfinished handwriting. Retention is 1, 7 or 30 days from the last checkpoint; expiry runs when the app next checks recovery. Turning recovery off stops new checkpoints but does not immediately delete existing copies. Use the deletion controls to remove them. Verified export or explicit discard removes that session’s recovery copy.

Passwords and page text

PDF passwords stay in session memory and are not saved in drafts or recovery. Reopened work may require the password again. Page text is extracted locally into session memory and cleared when dismissed or the page or document changes. It is not sent to an external reading service and does not use image text recognition.

Authentication and backups

The optional saved-data lock uses Android device authentication. The app does not receive your fingerprint, face template or passcode. The encryption key itself is not authentication-bound. Private app data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Exported files and copies held by your selected providers are outside these exclusions.

Google Play

Google Play handles lifetime Pro purchases, optional auto-renewing monthly Pro subscriptions, restoration and subscription management. The app receives product, purchase-token, purchase-state and signed purchase information to verify access and acknowledge purchases. Verified records, signatures and recent ownership-check times are kept in app-private preferences, separately from the encrypted signing vault. Monthly access can require a fresh Play check after the limited offline allowance; cancellation retains access while Play reports the paid entitlement. The app does not receive payment-card details or pass document data to billing. Google handles transaction data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Optional rewarded advertising

If you choose Watch an ad, Google AdMob and its consent service prepare a rewarded video. Completing the reward unlocks bulk initials for that document on this device; manual signing and clean export remain free. Pro does not request reward ads. Advertising is not started on ordinary app launch; choosing the ad flow can start SDK activity for the current app process.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device/account identifiers, interaction and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent choices and availability depend on your region and Google configuration. Where required, the consent form is shown before ads can be requested; Ad privacy choices in Privacy reopens available choices. Declining or unavailable ads does not prevent manual signing or export. We do not attach PDF contents, names, extracted text, signatures or stamp images to ad requests. Other data may still be handled by the SDK; this is not a claim that no data leaves the device.

The app keeps earned unlocks in encrypted local storage using a fingerprint of the source PDF. That fingerprint is not passed to advertising services. Reopening the same source, including a saved draft or recovery copy, retains the unlock. Deleting earned document unlocks in Privacy removes these records; it does not remove already placed initials or paid Pro access. Editing the original file outside the app creates different source bytes and may no longer match the unlock.

Google's data-handling information: Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Other apps and providers

A storage provider or recipient you select receives files when you save or share, and may use its own network and backups. Deleting local data does not remove exported or shared copies. Android, your keyboard and enabled accessibility services may process information under their own settings. The app requests reduced keyboard learning for sensitive entries, but cannot guarantee every keyboard’s behaviour.

Delete your data

Privacy includes deletion controls for saved signatures, initials, stamps, drafts, Pro data, recent-file history, recovery sessions and earned document unlocks. Discarding a document removes its active working session. Android app-storage controls or uninstalling remove local app data; keep finished PDFs you need first. Restoring a Play purchase can recreate the entitlement record. There is no Singularity Shift app account or hosted document copy to delete. Delete exported files through their storage provider and contact recipients about shared copies.

Support enquiries

If you email us, we receive your address, message and attachments. We use these to respond and handle support, privacy or purchase enquiries. Our email provider processes correspondence for delivery and storage. Avoid sending confidential PDFs or signatures when a description or redacted example will do. Correspondence is retained only as needed for the enquiry and applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent, subject to applicable law.

Changes and contact

We will identify an effective date and update this policy when data handling changes. Questions: singularityshiftai@gmail.com.
