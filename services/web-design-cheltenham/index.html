Considered from the start

The details matter.

We agree the work your website needs. That can include:

Content and structure
Make the services, proof of work and next step easy to find. Work with your existing copy and assets, and identify what is missing.
Design and mobile usability
A visual style that suits the business, with readable type, clear navigation and layouts that adapt to different screens.
Search foundations
Descriptive page titles, meaningful content, crawlable links, a sitemap and appropriate metadata. These help discovery; they do not guarantee rankings.
Development and checks
Build the agreed pages and interactions, then check layout, navigation, links and the main enquiry journey before launch.
Handover and next steps
Agree how the site is hosted, how content will be updated and what help you will need after launch. Recurring costs and support belong in the proposal.

Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

Website, web app or bespoke software? →
Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. Send the URL and describe what is not working for you. I’ll look at the current setup before recommending targeted improvements or a rebuild.

How much will my website cost?

Pricing is bespoke. The pages, content, design work, features and existing setup all affect the scope. We agree what is included and the cost before paid work starts. Read the website budgeting guide.

Do I need all the content ready?

A rough outline is enough for an initial conversation. Share the material you have; we can identify the copy, images and decisions needed for the agreed build.

What about hosting and future updates?

We discuss these during scoping, including who manages the site, any recurring costs and whether you need ongoing support. They are agreed as part of the proposal.

Do you only work in Cheltenham?

I’m based in Cheltenham and work with clients across the UK. You deal directly with me throughout the project.
