Is a small improvement worth discussing?

Yes. An awkward workflow, one integration or a contained fix can be a useful project in its own right. We can scope that without committing to a larger rebuild.

How do you price custom software?

Every engagement is priced individually. I’ll explain the scope, assumptions and cost before paid work begins. Where the unknowns are significant, the first proposal may be for a focused investigation.

Do I need to specify the technology?

No. Explain the task, who uses the system and where the problems appear. Share details of existing tools if you have them; the technical approach can follow from that.

Will I need AI?

Only if it is a useful fit. Many improvements are better served by a clear interface, a reliable integration or conventional automation. Where AI is appropriate, we discuss its limits and the review it needs.

What should I send you?

A short outline of the problem, the existing system or website, and any budget or timing constraints. Please leave passwords, access keys and private customer records out of the initial email.
